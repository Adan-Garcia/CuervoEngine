# Code of Conduct

Cuervo Engine is built by people with different backgrounds, skills and opinions. This
code applies to every project space: the repository, its issues and pull requests,
discussions, chat, and events where someone represents the project.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give and accept technical criticism constructively, and explain the reasons behind a
  review comment or a decision.
- Welcome newcomers, and answer questions without condescension.
- Respect people's time and boundaries: stay on topic, and don't demand work from
  volunteers.
- Credit other people's work, and respect its licenses.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, or threats, in public or in private.
- Insults, slurs, or derogatory comments about anyone's identity, background, or
  ability.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions, or trolling.
- Anything else that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behavior that breaks this code, tell the maintainers privately.
Use the contact details on the repository owner's profile, or open a private security
advisory on the repository if you need a confidential channel. Reports are handled in
confidence. Only the people who need to know see the details, and the reporter is not
identified without their consent. A maintainer who is part of a report is kept out of
handling it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers decide what to do based on what happened and its impact. The response can
range from a private conversation or a public warning to a temporary or permanent ban
from project spaces. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, and issues that
break this code. A maintainer who doesn't follow the code may be removed from the role.

This code is adapted in spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
