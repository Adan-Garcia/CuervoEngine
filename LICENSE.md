## A word before the legal text

Cuervo Engine is built so that people can make games with it, learn from it, change it and
share what they learn. We want a community around it that feels like any good open
project: you can read the source, fork it, send patches and make mods.

So here is the deal in plain English:

- **It is free for hobbyists, students, indie developers, and commercial creators during Beta.**
  While the Engine is in Beta (prior to the Version 1.0 release), you pay nothing. No royalties,
  no seat fees, no forms, as many people on your team as you like.
- **It is free to learn, mod and fork,** as long as the fork doesn't make money.
- **Commercial pricing will be introduced at Version 1.0, with grandfathering for Beta users.**
  When the Engine reaches Version 1.0, commercial licensing tiers will be introduced to support
  ongoing development. However, **any game or Product developed or released using a Beta version
  of the Engine is grandfathered in under these Beta terms ($0 fees and royalties)** and will not
  owe retroactive fees. Future commercial terms will apply only if you choose to upgrade your
  Product to Version 1.0 or later.
- **It can't be dodged by rewriting the code.** If it runs on our engine, changed or not,
  the same terms apply. Your own game's code and assets are always entirely yours.
- **Contributions are welcome.** Patches you send back help everyone, and the
  contribution terms below keep that simple and safe for you and for us.

This license exists to pay for the engine's development, not to squeeze developers.
The legal text below is more formal because it has to be exact. If something in it feels
unfair to a developer or team, that is a bug in our drafting, so please tell us at
`contact@adangarcia.com`.

**This is a source-available license, not an open-source license.** It is not approved by
