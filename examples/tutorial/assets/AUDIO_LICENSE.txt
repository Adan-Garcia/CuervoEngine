Audio in this directory (sfx/pickup.wav, sfx/hurt.wav)
======================================================

Copied from examples/shmup/assets/sfx (pickup.wav is shoot.wav renamed).
Every sound there is synthesized by tools/gen_shmup_audio.py from oscillators
and seeded noise; no recordings or third-party samples are involved.
