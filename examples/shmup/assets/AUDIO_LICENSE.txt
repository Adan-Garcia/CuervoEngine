Audio in this directory (sfx/*.wav, music/stage_loop.wav)
=========================================================

Every sound here is synthesized by tools/gen_shmup_audio.py from oscillators
and seeded noise; no recordings or third-party samples are involved. Rerunning
the script reproduces the files.
